    Frequently asked questions

    What is Void Links?

    A free link tool with two parts: short links you can lock, schedule and track, and a profile page at voids.wtf/yourname for everything you share.

    Is it free?

    Yes. Everything you see here is free while Void Links is in beta.

    Do I need an account to shorten a link?

    No. You can shorten a link right away. Sign up when you want your own page, a dashboard and click stats.

    What can I put on my page?

    A photo, a background image, GIF or video, music, your links with icons, a custom cursor, and what you're listening to on Spotify or doing on Discord.

    How do I show what I'm listening to?

    Connect Spotify in the page editor, or add your Discord ID (after joining the Lanyard Discord server) so every visitor sees your live status.

    What do the click stats show?

    Clicks per day, where visitors came from, their country and device, plus page views. Each visitor counts once a day, so refreshing doesn't pump the numbers.

    Is it safe?

    Pages and links can be reported, and every report is reviewed. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider, and API keys are only stored as fingerprints, never in full.

    How long does setup take?

    About a minute: sign up, pick your name, add a few links and save.
